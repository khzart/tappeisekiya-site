About the Brand

Since 1974 in Okayama,
CAETLA Co., Ltd. has pursued lace and embroidery for half a century.

TAPPEI SEKIYA was born
as the culmination of that aesthetic.

From the place where inherited craftsmanship
meets contemporary beauty.

A single thread, millimeter-scale devotion,
and handwork carrying the weight of time.

The garments that emerge resonate with the wearer,
becoming singular sculptures that cast a quiet light.

Lace is not decoration.

Designer Tappei Sekiya sees the time held in each stitch
as a form shaped by the body, light, air, and silence.

That is why CAETLA's craft, through his name,
is renewed as clothing for a new era.

PHILOSOPHY
Philosophy
Why is this lace here.
Why does this thread run in this direction.
A garment should not be an answer, but a question.
Something that leaves a quiet inquiry within the one who wears it.
Only that transcends time.
